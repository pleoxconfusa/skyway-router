Real streets, modeled skyway hours. The basemap and the walking + skyway network are live OpenStreetMap — outdoor routing follows real sidewalks/streets, and skyways are drawn on real geometry. Skyway hours stay modeled (no live per-door feed; each building sets its own and they change): city ordinance (M–F 6:30a–10p · Sat 9:30a–8p · Sun 12–6p) or typical (most close ~6p, weekends shut), switchable; hospital/hotel zones run 24h. Skyway segments are identified from OSM tags — level/layer≥1, indoor corridors (indoor=yes/corridor), building passages, and skyway tags — so building-internal concourses count, not just bridges. Through-buildings links skyway ends that share an OSM building footprint (lobbies/concourses you pass through, like the Ruth’s Chris entrance) plus a street door into each skyway-connected building \u2014 toggleable. If OSM can’t load, it falls back to a modeled grid. Tiles/search are OSM/Carto/Nominatim (rate-limited). Watch the signs.
